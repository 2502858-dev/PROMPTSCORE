How it works

A good prompt saves time. A vague one costs you rewrites. PromptScore shows you which one you have before you spend a request on it.

  1. 1. Paste your promptAdd the prompt exactly as you would send it. If you want, say what it is for and what the AI should accomplish, so the review judges it against your real goal.
  2. 2. Read your scoreYou get a score out of 10 and a grade, with a short explanation of the biggest strength and the biggest weakness in the prompt.
  3. 3. Use the rewriteCopy the improved prompt, or load it back into the editor and score it again to see how much the changes helped.

What gets scored

Every prompt is judged on five qualities, worth 20 points each. The total is divided by ten to give your score out of 10.

ClarityIs the goal precise and easy to act on, or could it be read in several ways?20
ContextDoes the prompt give the background the task needs, such as the audience, the field, and the material to work from?20
Specifics and limitsAre the scope, rules, and signs of a good result stated?20
Output formatDoes it say what the answer should look like: length, structure, tone, and detail?20
TechniqueIs the approach right for the job? Simple tasks do not need complex prompts.20

What your score means

Use the grade as a guide to how much work the prompt still needs.

  • 9.0 to 10Excellent
  • 7.5 to 8.9Very good
  • 6.0 to 7.4Good
  • 4.0 to 5.9Developing
  • 0 to 3.9Needs significant improvement

Common questions

Will the same prompt always get the same score?

Scores can shift slightly between runs because the review is written by an AI model.

Can I raise my score by asking for a high one?

No. Only the quality of the prompt counts.

Does a short prompt score badly?

Not by itself. A short prompt can score very well when the task is simple.

Are my prompts saved?

Scored prompts may be saved to your account. Avoid pasting private or confidential information.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in to score prompts, keep your results, and take part in the leaderboard.
